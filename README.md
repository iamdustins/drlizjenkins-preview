## 🌟 Interactive AI Features Included

1. **Neuro-Inclusive AI Conversation & Conflict Analyzer**
   - Decodes neurodivergent relationship dynamics (ADHD executive dysfunction & task paralysis, Autistic emotional buffering/flat-affect, Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria / RSD, and Double-Neurodivergent sensory collisions).
   - Generates Dr. Liz's clinical 3-lens breakdown:
     1. *What’s Happening In The Neurodivergent Brain*
     2. *The Partner’s Legitimate Emotional Reality*
     3. *The Neuro-Bridge Reconnect Script*

2. **AI "Say This, Not That" De-Escalation Translator**
   - Live interactive widget that takes heated phrases (blame, exhaustion) and rewrites them into vulnerable, connection-first statements that disarm partner defensiveness.

3. **60-Second Relationship Clarity Pulse**
   - An interactive 4-step diagnostic that identifies the couple's current dynamic (*Roommate Drift*, *High-Reactivity Cycle*, *Pursuer-Withdrawer*, *Flame-Ready*) and prescribes an immediate action step.

4. **Dr. Liz 24/7 AI Connection Concierge**
   - Floating chat assistant trained on Dr. Liz's books, philosophies, and neuro-affirming principles with ethical therapeutic guardrails.

5. **The Marriage Reset Book Showcase**
   - Chapter 1 interactive excerpt modal, Amazon purchase links, and audio welcome player.
