Reframed Vulnerable Script Step 1: Repair

"I’ve really missed you today and I crave some quiet connection with you. Could we take 15 minutes together after dinner to just be together without our screens?"

Why it works: Replaces the trigger word "always" with vulnerable desire, removing defensiveness.

Neuro-Affirming Communication Breakdown

Understanding how ADHD executive function and sensory overwhelm affect couple communication.

What Happened:

Partner forgot an urgent promised phone call; other partner feels completely unvalued and explodes.

Compassionate Reframing:
The Brain Reality: This is executive dysfunction / working memory drop, not intentional neglect. Calling it "lack of love" causes shame.
The Partner's Feeling: The partner carries repeated cognitive load and feels like a parent rather than an equal lover.
Suggested Bridge Script: "I know you didn't forget on purpose, but I'm feeling stressed carrying this alone. Can we take 2 minutes right now and do it together?"

The Marriage Reset — Chapter 1

"You’re Not Broken. You’re Just Out of Sync."

"I have sat across from thousands of couples over thirty-eight years. Couples who arrived in silence, clutching their coffee cups like shields, convinced that the love they once felt had died quietly in its sleep.

Here is what I tell them on day one: Love does not die quietly. It gets buried under the laundry, the unanswered texts, the defensiveness of exhaustion, and the silent assumption that our partner can read our minds.

When couples tell me, 'We just fight constantly,' I look beneath the surface. Anger is almost never the primary emotion. Anger is the bodyguard of grief. Beneath 'Why didn’t you take out the trash?' is almost always: 'Do you still see me? Do I still matter to you?'"
