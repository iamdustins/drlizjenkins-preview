Option 2: Boundary with Emotional Reassurance

"I love you and I care about our family, but it hurts when you say I never help. I'm tied up with a work call until 5:15 PM, but I can handle pickup right after that."

Option 3: Time-Out & Nervous System Reset

"I notice we're both getting tense and defensive over text. Can we pause for 10 minutes and talk over the phone? I want to make sure we're on the same team."
